title: Discounts and promo codes
---

## Seasonal sales

We run two sales a year: an **end-of-winter sale** in March and a **Black Friday sale** in November. Sale prices are shown on the product page and in the basket. Outside these sales, we don't run standing discounts on any product type (for example, there is no permanent discount on hats).

## Promo codes

Promo codes are sent by email to newsletter subscribers. You can use **one promo code per order**, and codes can't be combined with sale prices unless the code says so.

## Student and other group discounts

We don't offer student, military or senior discounts at the moment.

## Price changes after you order

We don't refund the difference if a price drops after you have placed your order.
