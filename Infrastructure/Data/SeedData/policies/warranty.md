## Warranty length by product type

Every product we sell comes with a manufacturer's warranty from the day it was delivered:

| Product type | Warranty |
|---|---|
| Boards | 2 years |
| Boots | 1 year |
| Gloves | 6 months |
| Hats | 6 months |

We only sell snowboards, boots, gloves and hats. We don't sell laptops, phones or other electronics, so we don't offer warranties for them.

## What the warranty covers

Faults in materials or how the item was made, for example a delaminating board core, a broken boot lace hook, split seams or a failed zip.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Normal wear and tear, such as scratched bases, worn edges or faded colours.
- Damage from accidents, crashes, misuse or wrong repairs.
- Items bought from other shops.
- Lost or stolen items.

## How to make a warranty claim

Email support with your order number, a short description of the fault and a photo. If the claim is accepted, we repair or replace the item. If neither is possible, we refund the price you paid.
