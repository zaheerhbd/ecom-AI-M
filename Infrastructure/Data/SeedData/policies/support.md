title: Customer support and store locations
---

## How to contact us

Email our support team at **support@skinet-shop.example**. We answer within one business day.

## Opening hours

Support is open **Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 Eastern Time**, except public holidays in the United States.

## Store locations

We are an **online-only** shop. We have no physical stores or showrooms, in New York or anywhere else, and no click-and-collect.

## What support can help with

Orders and delivery, returns and refunds, warranty claims, payment problems and product questions such as sizing.
