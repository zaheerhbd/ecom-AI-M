title: Payment methods
---

## Accepted payment methods

We accept card payments only: **Visa, Mastercard and American Express**, credit or debit. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

## Payment methods we don't accept

- **No cash on delivery.** Every order is paid online at checkout.
- **No cryptocurrency** (for example Bitcoin or Ethereum).
- No bank transfers, cheques, gift cards or buy-now-pay-later services.

## When you are charged

Your card is charged when you place the order. All prices are in US dollars. If your bank uses another currency, it converts the amount at its own rate and may add a fee.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the order is not placed and you are not charged. Check your card details or try another card. Some banks ask you to confirm the payment in their app first.
