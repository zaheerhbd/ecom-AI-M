---
title: Returns and refunds policy
---

## Return window

You can return any item within **30 days** of the day it was delivered. After 30 days we can't accept a return or give a refund. If an item stops working after 30 days, check our warranty policy instead: faults may still be covered.

## Condition of returned items

Items must be unused, in their original packaging and with all tags attached. Snowboards must not have been mounted with bindings or ridden. Hats and gloves must be clean and unworn.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in and open **My orders**.
2. Email support with your order number and the items you want to return.
3. We reply within one business day with a return label and instructions.

Return shipping is free for faulty or wrong items. For any other return, the cost of the label is taken from your refund.

## Refund timing

Once your return reaches us, we check it within 3 business days. The refund then goes back to the card you paid with. Your bank usually shows it within **5 to 10 business days**. Original delivery costs are refunded only if the item was faulty or wrong.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, email support within **7 days** of delivery with your order number and a photo. We send a free replacement or a full refund, including delivery costs. This applies to international orders as well. Import duties and taxes paid to your country's customs can't be refunded by us; you may be able to claim them back from your local customs office.
