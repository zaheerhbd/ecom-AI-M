title: Shipping and international delivery
---

## Delivery options

You choose a delivery option at checkout. The fastest option (UPS1) takes **1 to 2 days**. We don't offer next-day or same-day delivery. The other options are cheaper but slower, and the free option takes 1 to 2 weeks. Delivery times start when the order leaves our warehouse, usually within one business day.

## Countries we ship to

We ship to the **United States, Canada and the United Kingdom**. We don't ship to other countries yet. The delivery times shown at checkout are for the United States; orders to Canada and the United Kingdom usually take **7 to 14 business days** longer.

## Duties and taxes

For orders outside the United States, your country may charge import duties and taxes when the parcel arrives. These are **not included** in our prices and are paid by you to the courier or customs office. We can't predict the exact amount, and we can't refund it.

## Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number by email once it ships.
